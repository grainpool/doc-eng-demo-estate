## My upload was rejected

Two distinct rejections exist: unsupported type (only `.csv` and `.tsv` are
accepted) and size (see [Supported files](/supported-files) for the limit).
The error message names which one you hit.

## Relay refused my question

The eight-operation catalog is closed by design. A refusal lists supported
alternatives; rephrasing toward one of them usually succeeds. Refusals are
not errors — they are the feature working.

## "The analysis engine is unavailable"

The kernel cold-starts after idle periods; the first question can take a few
extra seconds, and a transient failure is retried once automatically. If the
error persists, check the deployment's health page.

## Why does the app say iOS is unsupported when the changelog announced it?

The 1.5.0 iOS launch was rolled back after release. Release notes record
history; configuration records the present. When they disagree, the
configuration is correct — a standing decision record documents this
tie-break.
