description: "Canonical product terms: Task, Project, Artifact — and the words Relay deliberately avoids."
owner: "docs"
---

{/* concord:fact key=term.canonical.project */}
**Project** — the container for files, sessions, and artifacts.

{/* concord:fact key=term.canonical.task */}
**Task** — a unit of analysis work. Renamed from "Job" in release 1.3.0;
"Job" no longer appears anywhere in the product.

{/* concord:fact key=term.canonical.artifact */}
**Artifact** — a durable analysis output with complete provenance.

## Words we avoid

"Notebook" (Relay is not one), "script" (nothing user-authored executes),
and "AI insights" (results are statistics with provenance, not generated
claims).
