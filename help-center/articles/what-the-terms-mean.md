# What the terms mean

- **Project** — the workspace holding your files, sessions, and artifacts.
- **Task** — one unit of analysis work (you might see this in the CLI and API).
- **Artifact** — any saved result, with its full origin story attached.
- **Session** — the conversation where you ask questions about one file.
- **Lineage** — the chain from a result back through the results it was computed from.
